## Denoise and responsiveness, separately

- Classic is VST/wavelets; AI is Apple's RAW denoise stand-in, with no bundled learned model. Assess luma/chroma/detail/hot-pixel behavior on each camera across ISO, particularly GX85 high ISO. Both cameras currently use generic full-frame ISO noise seeds; synthetic MSE improvement is not camera calibration.
- Judge noise reduction against retained fine texture/color and spatial artifacts, not noise variance alone. Zero-strength identity, mathematical response and subjective quality are separate claims.
- Measure native first-frame/sample latency, warmed slider-to-completed-frame latency and memory on the owner's hardware. Report actual completed frames and timing conditions. Cloud shared-process timings and process-cold command RSS do not establish cold-disk or GPU memory bounds.

Acceptance does not imply every feature or aesthetic quality is certified. Preserve unresolved failures and blocked checks alongside passes.

## 1. Texture

**Expected math (consensus).** Adobe's own engineering statement (Max Wendt, lead engineer): Texture is a *mid-frequency* band adjustment — sharpening works the high-frequency band, Texture isolates medium-sized detail so it can smooth skin without erasing pores or boost bark/hair without amplifying noise or touching bokeh ([Adobe: From the ACR Team — Introducing the Texture Control](https://theblog.adobe.com/from-the-acr-team-introducing-the-texture-control/), [Julieanne Kost, Adobe](https://blogs.adobe.com/jkost/2019/05/the-texture-slider-in-lightroom-classic.html), [DPReview coverage](https://www.dpreview.com/news/7313066292/adobe-adds-new-texture-slider-to-lightroom-cc-lightroom-classic-and-acr)). Adobe documents *which band*, not the decomposition; that a multi-scale (wavelet/pyramid) band gain is the standard realization is **inferred** — the undecimated à-trous transform is the textbook tool for exact-reconstruction band gains ([Auchère, à-trous wavelets reference implementation](https://github.com/frederic-auchere/wavelets/)). Negative Texture ≈ mild NR is documented in the Adobe post.

**What Lumen does.**
- *Reference:* gain on the mid scales of a 5-level à-trous B3-spline stack of log2 luminance (`Sources/LumenCore/Image/DetailEngine.swift:251-309`; stack built at `Sources/LumenCore/Image/SpatialOps.swift:360-370`). Raised-cosine window over scale index, halfWidth 1.6 (`DetailEngine.swift:271,925-930`), centre at level 1 ≈ 4 px shifted by `log2(longEdge/2560)` for scale honesty (`DetailEngine.swift:904-908`), truncation-normalized so fit view and export get equal energy (`DetailEngine.swift:279-284,913-921`). Both signs gated by structure-tensor coherence — negative by `1−coherence` (skin smoother, edge-preserving), positive by a smoothstep gate opening 0.35–0.85 so hair/weave (coherence ≈ 0.19) keep full authority while hard steps close it (`DetailEngine.swift:94-99,294-304`). Applied as ΔEV via one hue-safe luma-ratio multiply (`DetailEngine.swift:875-889`).
- *Shipping GPU:* **one band**, not a stack: image minus a single self-guided filter base at radius `max(0.003·longEdge, 2)` (`Sources/LumenPipeline/RenderGraph.swift:556,599`), gain `k = texture/100 · 0.9 · perStop` (`RenderGraph.swift:597-601`), ε unit-converted from `DetailEngine.baseEpsilon = 0.01` EV² into the LumenLog domain (`RenderGraph.swift:580`; `DetailEngine.swift:76`), coherence gate at the reference's radius (`RenderGraph.swift:617-631`), gain kernel `exp2(k·(hi−lo))` (`Sources/LumenPipeline/Kernels.swift:105-110`).

**Verdict: DIVERGES (graph vs reference), defensible in kind, unproven in magnitude.** The one-band form is a *high-pass* (all scales finer than the radius, including the 1-px noise scale at full weight) where the reference is a *band-pass* whose raised-cosine window carries only ~0.31 weight at level 0 (inferred from `bandWeight` at `DetailEngine.swift:925-930`) — so the shipping Texture is spectrally noisier per unit of effect than the reference, and the 0.9 gain constant against the reference's normalized 1.0 is a calibration guess. The unit fix (the 1/24 LumenLog error that made Texture do ~2.6% of its stated effect) is recorded at `RenderGraph.swift:582-596`, but the post-fix band-magnitude match between paths is explicitly unmeasured. Gate parity was measured and fixed (`Kernels.swift:255-274`).

## 2. Clarity

**Expected math (consensus).** Clarity is midtone local contrast; Adobe's Lightroom 4-era Clarity/Shadows/Highlights are publicly tied to local Laplacian filtering: [Paris, Hasinoff, Kautz, "Local Laplacian Filters" (SIGGRAPH 2011)](https://people.csail.mit.edu/sparis/publi/2011/siggraph/Paris_11_Local_Laplacian_Filters.pdf) and the fast variant [Aubry, Paris, Hasinoff, Kautz, Durand (TOG 2014)](https://people.csail.mit.edu/hasinoff/pubs/AubryEtAl14-lapfilters.pdf), whose abstract states the technique's use in Adobe's products. The per-γ monotone remap `r(x) = γ + sign(d)·σ·(|d|/σ)^α` with a small set of sampled γ levels is darktable's shipped parameterization ([darktable: local laplacian pyramids](https://www.darktable.org/2017/11/local-laplacian-pyramids/)). Documented. The "large-radius midtone-contrast mask" description of older Clarity is community-level history ([Keptlight on LR4 Clarity](https://www.keptlight.com/lightroom-4-clarity-slider/)) — secondary.

**What Lumen does.**
- *Reference:* real fast local Laplacian on log2 luminance: 6 sampled remap levels (darktable's count, `DetailEngine.swift:684`), remap exactly the Paris/Aubry point function (`DetailEngine.swift:741-748`), `α = max(1 − 0.7·amount·w, 0.05)` with a Gaussian midtone weight of width 3.0 EV on γ (`DetailEngine.swift:701-706,82`), σ = 0.7 EV detail/edge threshold (`DetailEngine.swift:79`), coarsest level copied so white/black points survive (`DetailEngine.swift:732-734`), pyramid depth tracks the frame one level per doubling (`DetailEngine.swift:69-73`). Output as ΔEV luma ratio (`DetailEngine.swift:360-363`).
- *Shipping GPU:* **single guided band + remap**, not a pyramid: picture minus one self-guided base at radius `0.02·longEdge` (`RenderGraph.swift:557,638-639`), then the reference's own remap point function evaluated on that one band, with γ approximated by the pixel's encoded distance from mid-grey (`Kernels.swift:374-391`, wired at `RenderGraph.swift:661-665`). The ε rim fix (the guided filter's ε had been 68×–152× too large in the encoded domain, smearing 1.5 EV edges into the band) cut the measured halo on a 3 EV step from **0.72 EV → 0.108 EV** dark-side (0.70 → 0.092 bright-side) against the reference local Laplacian's 0.066/0.050, at equal texture gain 1.63 (`RenderGraph.swift:559-580`; single-band construction rationale `RenderGraph.swift:640-660`).

**Verdict: DIVERGES (shipping is a one-band remap where the spec and reference are local Laplacian), defensible and measured.** Residual bright/dark rim ~2× the reference's, within 0.05 EV absolute; the kernel's own comment states honestly why the pyramid cannot be matched by one band (`Kernels.swift:370-373`). "Punch" mode (chroma at ~35%) is specified but not on the wire (`DetailEngine.swift:337-340`). Reference construction is CORRECT against the published algorithm.

## 3. Dehaze

**Expected math (consensus).** Haze model `I = J·t + A·(1−t)`; dark channel prior; `t = 1 − ω·min_patch(min_c I_c/A_c)` with ω = 0.95 haze retention; airlight = scene color at the brightest 0.1% of the dark channel; transmission floored (t₀ = 0.1 in the paper) and edge-refined — [He, Sun, Tang, CVPR 2009](https://projectsweb.cs.washington.edu/research/insects/CVPR2009/award/hazeremv_drkchnl.pdf), [IEEE record](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/5206515/). Documented. Guided-filter refinement of t is He's own later practice ([He, Sun, Tang, guided filter ECCV 2010/TPAMI 2013](http://kaiminghe.com/eccv10/)). Adobe describes Dehaze as "based on a physical model of how light is transmitted… estimates light lost to absorption and scattering" ([Envato Tuts+, quoting Adobe](https://photography.tutsplus.com/tutorials/how-to-use-the-adobe-lightroom-cc-dehaze-tool--cms-30198); community discussion [Lightroom Queen thread](https://www.lightroomqueen.com/community/threads/so-what-is-the-dehaze-actually-doing.25853/)). **Attribution note:** the task brief names Eric Chan for Adobe's dehaze; I could not verify a primary Eric Chan source in this session — treat that attribution as unconfirmed.

**What Lumen does.**
- *Reference:* full He/Sun/Tang on scene-linear: dark channel radius `min(w,h)/100` (`DetailEngine.swift:186-187,205-206`; erosion `SpatialOps.swift:398-406`), airlight = mean of the top 0.1% of the dark channel read from the source (`SpatialOps.swift:416-476`), `t = 1 − 0.95·dark(I/A)` per-channel-normalized, guided-refined with the shared log-luminance node, clamped [0.02, 1] (`DetailEngine.swift:199-217,101`). Recovery diverges from the paper deliberately: the veil is first neutralized (white-balanced against A), the inversion runs on **luminance only**, and the scalar ratio (clamped [0.05, 20], with a relative-trust fade for near-zero-luminance colors) multiplies the untouched RGB — hue-preserving by construction, the stated magenta-sky fix (`DetailEngine.swift:370-452`). Transmission floor `mix(0.55, 0.05, Distance=20/100)` plus a bright-and-flat **sky guard** lifting the floor toward 0.9 (`DetailEngine.swift:410-421,103`). Negative Dehaze runs the model forward with distance estimated from `max(1−t, skyness)` (`DetailEngine.swift:453-474`).
- *Shipping GPU:* same construction via stock morphology + kernels (`RenderGraph.swift:675-819`, kernel `Kernels.swift:138-164`): short-edge-sized window (`RenderGraph.swift:678-682`), He-correct airlight as masked mean of source where the dark-channel proxy is brightest (`RenderGraph.swift:695-738`), per-channel normalization and saturation before t (`RenderGraph.swift:739-778`), log-luminance-guided refinement at converted ε (`RenderGraph.swift:780-794`). **Two admitted gaps:** the sky guard is reference-only (kernel lacks the gradient/log-lum planes — `RenderGraph.swift:796-799`, `Kernels.swift:135-137`), and the negative branch's `distant = 1−raw` omits the reference's `skyness` term (`Kernels.swift:157-161` vs `DetailEngine.swift:470-471`).

**Verdict: CORRECT in family (documented DCP math, with a defensible, clearly-argued hue-preserving recombination that intentionally departs from He's per-channel inversion); GPU DIVERGES from reference on skies** — positive Dehaze on the GPU can strip distant atmosphere the reference would protect, and negative Dehaze distributes synthetic haze without the sky signal. Both divergences are self-documented; magnitude on real skies unmeasured in code comments.

## 4. Sharpen (Amount / Radius / Detail / Masking + Halo Suppression)

**Expected math (consensus).** Lightroom's four-slider contract: Amount = gain; Radius = edge width in pixels; Detail = a continuum whose high end mixes in *deconvolution*-style fine-detail sharpening (Adobe's Eric Chan has confirmed the deconvolution blend in community fora); Masking = an edge mask, 0 = uniform, 100 = restricted to strongest edges — [Adobe Help: sharpening sliders](https://helpx.adobe.com/ph_fil/lightroom-cc/how-to/sharpen-photos.html) (Documented for Radius/Detail/Masking semantics), [Lightroom Queen: Deconvolution](https://www.lightroomqueen.com/community/threads/deconvolution.36819/), [Greg Benz on deconvolution sharpening](https://gregbenzphotography.com/photography-tips/what-is-deconvolution-sharpening-and-how-to-get-increased-detail/) (the Detail-slider-as-deconvolution-blend claim is secondary/attributed, not in Adobe's docs). Halo suppression is Capture One's control; C1 documents the slider's purpose ([Capture One support](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002606917-Adjusting-sharpening-in-the-Sharpening-tool)); the classic mechanism in the patent literature is clamping overshoot to the local min/max ([US 2008/0298712, Image sharpening with halo suppression](https://patents.justia.com/patent/20080298712)). Unsharp mask as base operator: standard ([RawPedia: Sharpening](https://rawpedia.rawtherapee.com/Sharpening)).

**What Lumen does.** Reference and GPU are the same math (`DetailEngine.swift:537-603`; `RenderGraph.swift:947-1007`; kernel `Kernels.swift:184-204`): log-luminance USM `lum − G(σ=radius)`, radius clamped 0.5–3.0; `v = amount·mix(usm, fine, detail)` where `fine = details[0] + 0.5·details[1]` of the à-trous stack (`DetailEngine.swift:575-579`; GPU rebuilds it exactly via B-spline passes, `RenderGraph.swift:956-989`, with the recorded backwards-Detail history at `:959-973`); halo suppression damps **only** positive (bright) deltas, ramping over usm ∈ [0.15, 0.60] EV (`DetailEngine.swift:588-592`); masking gates on structure-tensor gradient with thresholds `[0.02+0.10m, 0.10+0.25m]` EV/px, scale-honest via `structureRadius(longEdge)` (`DetailEngine.swift:593-598`; `RenderGraph.swift:990-995,1111-1113`); applied as luma ratio limited to ±2 EV (`DetailEngine.swift:602`, `Kernels.swift:209-214`). Fallback to stock `CIUnsharpMask` with Detail folded into radius (`RenderGraph.swift:999-1005`, `Recipe.swift:790-796`). Wire: amount 0–150, radius 0.5–3.0, detail default 25 (LR parity), masking, haloSuppression (`Sources/LumenCore/Recipe/Recipe.swift:754-813`).

**Verdict: DIVERGES in three specific ways.**
1. **Radius is in OUTPUT pixels — defect for judgment, not for export.** σ is applied in pixels of whatever the graph is rendering (`RenderGraph.swift:951,982`; `DetailEngine.swift:564,571`), with no `longEdge` scaling — unlike Texture/Clarity/Masking radii which are deliberately frame-relative. A radius judged on a 2560 px fit preview renders a physically smaller (frame-relative) effect in a 6000 px export. Lightroom's radius is also pixel-denominated, but it is *full-resolution* pixels in both preview and export, so LR's preview judgment transfers; Lumen's does not. (Inferred from code; no counter-measurement exists in-repo.)
2. **Detail ≈ deconvolution is an approximation by construction:** the "deconvolution-weighted component" is the two finest à-trous bands, not a deconvolution — self-declared (`DetailEngine.swift:544-548`). Defensible: matches LR's *observable* semantics (high Detail = finest-scale emphasis), does not match the attributed mechanism. Note `SpatialOps.richardsonLucy` exists and is uncalled by this stage.
3. **Halo suppression is not C1's mechanism:** progressive one-sided damping of bright overshoot vs the patent-family min/max clamp. Inspired-by is the claim made (`Recipe.swift:752-753`), and the asymmetry argument is coherent; parity with C1 output is unproven.
Masking semantics: CORRECT against Adobe's documented description (directionally; the exact threshold curve is Lumen's own).

## 5. Capture Sharpening

**Expected math (consensus).** Capture sharpening = early-pipeline restoration of optical/AA/demosaic softness, canonically Richardson–Lucy deconvolution against an estimated (often Gaussian) system PSF, on linear data, few iterations: [RawPedia: Capture Sharpening/Sharpening](https://rawpedia.rawtherapee.com/Sharpening) (RT ships RL capture sharpening on linear data early in the pipeline), [Strolls with my Dog: The Richardson–Lucy Algorithm](https://www.strollswithmydog.com/richardson-lucy-algorithm/) and [best single PSF for capture sharpening](https://www.strollswithmydog.com/what-is-the-best-single-deconvolution-psf-to-use-for-capture-sharpening/), [Wikipedia: Richardson–Lucy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richardson%E2%80%93Lucy_deconvolution). RL iteration `estimate ← estimate · (PSF ⊛ (observed / (PSF ⊛ estimate)))` — documented.

**What Lumen does.**
- *Reference (written, dormant):* `DetailEngine.captureSharpen` — RL at 8 iterations on luminance (`DetailEngine.swift:495-535,512`), correct RL update with non-negativity floor, division floor, 8× ratio clamp (`SpatialOps.swift:492-515`), auto-σ from the image's own 1-D local peaks: `σ = sqrt(1/ln(maxRatio))` at the 99.5th percentile, clamped 0.4–2.0 px (`SpatialOps.swift:518-612`), exposure-invariant log-gradient noise gate, strength scales the correction not the mix (`DetailEngine.swift:519-533`).
- *Shipping:* **none of that runs.** The RAW stage scales Apple's at-demosaic sharpener by `strengthFraction` and reads nothing else (`Sources/LumenPipeline/AppleRawSource.swift:165-183`; mapping `Recipe.swift:720-730`, `auto` is the on-switch). `capture.radius` **is a dead control**: stored on the wire (`Recipe.swift:702`), shown as a slider (`Sources/LumenApp/DetailPanel.swift:112-119`), read only by the uncalled `captureSharpen` — the panel itself now says "Radius is stored but not applied in this build" (`DetailPanel.swift:128-134`) and that `richardsonLucy`/`estimatePSFSigma`/`captureSharpen` "appear in no file under Tests/" (`DetailPanel.swift:60-77`).

**Verdict: UNPROVEN (reference), dead control (shipping).** The reference RL math matches the documented algorithm on inspection, but has no caller and no test — nothing has ever observed it produce a pixel. The shipping behavior (vendor sharpener × fraction) has no defined math to be correct *against*. The UI is honest about it, which is the one thing keeping this from being a defect of representation.

## 6. Denoise (luma/chroma + classic sub-controls)

**Expected math (consensus).** Profiled Poisson–Gaussian noise `var = a·signal + b` per (camera, ISO), variance-stabilize, denoise in wavelet domain, invert with the **exact unbiased** inverse: [darktable: denoise (profiled) manual](https://docs.darktable.org/usermanual/4.6/en/module-reference/processing-modules/denoise-profiled/) (profiled model, wavelet + NLM variants — documented), [Mäkitalo & Foi, optimal inversion of the (generalized) Anscombe transformation](https://webpages.tuni.fi/foi/invansc/index.html) ([paper](https://webpages.tuni.fi/foi/papers/OptGenAnscombeInverse-doublecolumn-preprint.pdf)) — documented. Per-scale noise σ of the B3-spline à-trous stack under unit white noise: Starck–Murtagh's measured table (first scales ≈ 0.889, 0.200, 0.086, …) — [Auchère wavelets repo documenting the Starck–Murtagh values](https://github.com/frederic-auchere/wavelets/) — documented. Sub-control semantics (Luminance Detail/Contrast, Color Detail/Smoothness) follow Lightroom's manual-NR contract: [Adobe community/help descriptions via LR docs](https://helpx.adobe.com/lightroom-classic/help/retouch-photos.html) — documented at the level of what each slider trades, not its curve.

**What Lumen does.** S3 in both paths: hot-pixel pass → generalized Anscombe VST → orthonormal Y0U0V0 rotation → per-scale edge-aware soft thresholding of a 5-level à-trous stack → guided-filter chroma blotch pass → inverse VST.
- Noise model + generic ISO seed anchors (a ∝ ISO, b ∝ ISO^1.5), with GPL provenance discipline for darktable's numbers stated (`Sources/LumenCore/Image/DenoiseEngine.swift:39-106,66-77`).
- VST forward and the Mäkitalo–Foi closed-form exact unbiased inverse, with the algebraic inverse blended in by `shrinkageFraction` so an unshrunk image doesn't inherit the debias pedestal (`DenoiseEngine.swift:276-381,709-716`) — the blend is Lumen's own device (inferred-correct, argued at `:322-340`), the full-strength inverse is the documented one.
- Per-scale σ table `[0.8907, 0.2007, 0.0856, 0.0413, 0.0205, 0.0103]` = the Starck–Murtagh values, with the normalization assumption stated (`DenoiseEngine.swift:477-484`). Basis is darktable's decorrelating Y0U0V0 in orthonormal form (`:489-495`).
- Slider mappings in one table (`DenoiseEngine.swift:389-404`): `k_L = 2.5·(luma/100)^p` anchored 1.7 @ 60 (`:602-611,439-456`); `k_C = 2.5·(chroma/100)^q` anchored 1.6 @ 25 (`:651-660`); Luminance Detail `1.5 − d/100` threshold multiplier; Luminance Contrast pulls coarse-band thresholds down; Color Detail = chroma edge protection; Color Smoothness pushes coarse chroma thresholds up ×(1+3·s) plus a guided blotch mix capped at 0.10 (`:863-892,460-466,806-833`). Soft (not hard) thresholding (`:916-939`). Hot Pixels: median-deviation gate `k = 2+10(1−h/100)²`, extremum-only (`:662-677,985-1037`).
- **The chromaK bound and the σ double-count** (`DenoiseEngine.swift:613-660` and `1650-1683`): measured, chromaMaxK 8.0 had annihilated the band by slider 25 (dead top three-quarters), and the ISO-adaptive Color default climbing to 40/55 scored *worse than not denoising* (RMS 3.300 vs 3.181 undenoised at ISO 6400) — because thresholds are already σ-denominated and σ already rises with ISO: the climb was gain adaptation applied twice. Fixed: ceiling 2.5σ, colorAnchors flattened to 10/20/25/30 (`:1678-1683`), every travel point now beats undenoised (RMS table at `:646-648`).
- GPU path reads the same `GPUPlan` numbers, pedestal-relative encoding for half-float (`DenoiseEngine.swift:1039-1105`; `RenderGraph.swift:271-380`; kernels `Kernels.swift:477-515`).
- AI mode: Tier 2 does not exist; `.ai` maps Amount to the RAW decoder's own NR as a declared stand-in (`Recipe.swift:815-872`), with auto-zero coupling of classic sliders (`DenoiseEngine.swift:1774-1780`).

**Verdict: CORRECT in framework (documented VST + exact unbiased inverse + Starck–Murtagh σ + darktable basis), with Lumen-original calibration curves that are named, bounded, and partially measured. One suspected residual defect:** `luminanceAnchors` (`DenoiseEngine.swift:1644-1648`) still climbs 0 → 25 → 40 with ISO while luma thresholds are σ-denominated — the *same* double-count argument that was measured and fixed for chroma. lumaK's own ground-truth table (`:574-595`) shows RMS already worse than undenoised at slider 50 on the ISO 6400 proof frame; the ISO 25600 default of 40 therefore likely resolves past its own optimum. Chroma was re-measured; luma was not. Suspected defect, UNPROVEN pending the equivalent measurement. Sub-control curves themselves are honest inventions (docs/07 §12.6 open calibration, stated at `DenoiseEngine.swift:16-19`).

## 7. Grain

**Expected math (consensus).** Physical film grain lives in *density*, arises from discrete developed silver crystals, is strongest at mid densities and vanishes at Dmin/Dmax, decorrelates across colour layers (blue record coarsest), and its pitch is a property of the emulsion magnified by enlargement — the rigorous model is a Boolean/stochastic-geometry process, resolution-independent by construction: [Newson, Faraj, Delon, Galerne, "Realistic Film Grain Rendering" (IPOL 2017)](https://www.ipol.im/pub/art/2017/192/) ([PDF](https://www.lirmm.fr/~nfaraj/publications/film_grain_ipol/2017_Newson_film_grain.pdf)) — documented. darktable's grain module: applied on lightness, coarseness/strength controls, midtone bias (grain less visible in shadows and highlights) — [darktable grain manual](https://docs.darktable.org/usermanual/4.2/en/module-reference/processing-modules/grain/) — documented. Lightroom exposes Amount/Size/Roughness ([Adobe Help: Effects](https://helpx.adobe.com/lightroom/web/edit-photos/apply-effects/adjust-effects.html)) with no published math — the √(p(1−p)) binomial amplitude envelope is **inferred** industry practice (it is the variance of a two-state density process), not Adobe documentation.

**What Lumen does.** Density-domain grain inside picture formation, both paths: `D = −log10(v)`, `p = D/Dmax`, amplitude `√(p(1−p))`, `out = 10^−(D + amp·n·amount)` (`Sources/LumenCore/Engine/ReferenceRenderer.swift:417-462`; GPU kernel `Kernels.swift:446-457`; density scale 0.12 at Amount 100, `Sources/LumenCore/Engine/FilmLab.swift:603`; scalar print-stage Dmax `FilmLab.swift:1116-1122`). Three decorrelated per-layer plates seeded independently, blue coarsest via sizeScale (0.8, 1.0, 2.0); monochrome stocks share one seed so B&W grain has no colour (`FilmLab.swift:652-672,171,248`; `ReferenceRenderer.swift:426-440`; GPU plate builder `Sources/LumenPipeline/PipelineRenderer.swift:977-1030`). Pitch is denominated at the gate in µm and anchored to the *print* — crop coarsens grain, resize shrinks it, exactly the darkroom relationship (`FilmLab.swift:685-741`); push/pull couples amount and pitch (`FilmLab.swift:638-650`). Plate: deterministic, tileable 4-octave value noise, exact-unit-variance by measurement, seed shared across paths (`FilmLab.swift:757-874,600`); encode scale 8 shared with the kernel by interpolation so the GPU/reference half-amplitude bug cannot recur (`FilmLab.swift:606-617`, `Kernels.swift:442-445`).

**Verdict: CORRECT against the physical conventions it claims (density domain, binomial envelope, layer decorrelation, print-anchored pitch — each argued and cross-path pinned); DIVERGES from the literature's grain *texture* model.** A smoothed value-noise plate is Gaussian-like and C¹; Newson et al.'s Boolean model produces the hard-edged, spatially clumped crystal statistics that make grain read as grain at high magnification. No Roughness control exists (plate spectrum is fixed); Size exists only via stock pitch × `FilmGrain.size` (`Sources/LumenCore/Recipe/RecipeLook.swift:385`). Defensible as a first-order model; texture character vs film scans is unmeasured.

## 8. Halation

**Expected math (consensus).** Halation is light penetrating the emulsion, reflecting off the base/pressure plate, and re-exposing the red-sensitive layer from behind — a warm red-orange bloom hugging clipped highlights, radius growing with source intensity; suppressed on stocks with anti-halation (remjet) backing, iconic on CineStill 800T where it is removed: [CineStill FAQ](https://cinestillfilm.com/pages/frequently-asked-questions), [Gelatin Labs explainer](https://gelatinlabs.com/halation-nation-respooling-motion-picture-film-and-cinestill/), [Dehancer: Halation and its simulation](https://www.dehancer.com/learn/articles/halation-in-dehancer) (documents red-layer-first physics and green-layer leakage shifting halation toward orange). Documented physics; specific radii/decay constants are per-implementation.

**What Lumen does.** `HalationProfile` (`FilmLab.swift:438-583`): first-bounce σ = 65 µm at the film gate, converted to render pixels via gate size — so radius is a physical property of the stock, not of the file; three bounces at σ·√k with geometric decay 0.5 (`:441-445,486-495`); per-channel strengths red-dominant (0.05, 0.015, 0) blended toward pure red by Redness (`:451,500-502`); highlight-energy reconstruction: smoothstep gate over the top 4 EV below clip, with up to +0.3 stop boost of clipped energy (`:541-552`); additive recombination `out = in + strength·H` in scene-linear (`:556-558`), placed after vignette and before picture formation with the physical ordering argued (`ReferenceRenderer.swift:97-109`, `RenderGraph.swift:133-137`). Reference stage `ReferenceRenderer.swift:385-415`; GPU stage `RenderGraph.swift:1182-1222` (blur through the shared, measured `gaussianBlur` — the ×3 sigma bug history at `:1194-1206`); GPU highlight gate is a hard pedestal `max(E−threshold,0)·boost` matched to the reference ramp at its half-power point, declared as a tolerance-bounded stand-in (`FilmLab.swift:560-576`, `Kernels.swift:422-427`).

**Verdict: CORRECT in mechanism (pre-curve, scene-linear, red-weighted, multi-bounce base scattering — matches the documented physics; constants are Lumen's own "measured" values with no external citation, so treat 65 µm / 0.5 decay / (0.05, 0.015, 0) as authored, not documented).** GPU DIVERGES from reference at the highlight gate by declared approximation. Exposure gap: the graph's accessor pins Size = 1.0 and stock Redness (`FilmLab.swift:1127-1134`) — Amount is the only live user control even though the profile supports more.

## 9. Vignette

**Expected math (consensus).** Natural (optical) vignetting follows the cosine-fourth illumination law ([Photonics Online: Breaking down the cosine fourth power law](https://www.photonicsonline.com/doc/breaking-down-the-cosine-fourth-power-law-0001), [Aggarwal et al., On Cosine-Fourth and Vignetting Effects in Real Lenses](https://vision.ai.illinois.edu/html-files-to-import/publications/on%20Cosine-fourth%20and%20Vignetting%20Effects%20in%20Real%20Lenses.pdf)) — but a *creative post-crop* vignette is a designed falloff, not cos⁴. The LR contract: Amount, Midpoint, Roundness, Feather, Highlights, with styles including Highlight Priority (recovers/blends highlights through the burn) — [Adobe Help / Julieanne Kost](https://jkost.com/blog/2024/08/adding-vignette-and-grain-effects-in-lightroom-classic.html), [Photofocus post-crop vignette guide](https://photofocus.com/software/finessing-a-post-crop-vignette-in-lightroom/). Documented at the control-surface level; Adobe publishes no curve.

**What Lumen does.** EV-denominated multiply on scene-linear RGB, −3.00…+1.00, before the display transform (`DetailEngine.swift:605-677`; wire `Sources/LumenCore/Recipe/RecipeLook.swift:14`). Falloff = `smoothstep(inner, 1, r)` with r normalized so the corner of the (crop) rectangle's inscribed ellipse is 1 (Roundness-0 geometry, both paths: `DetailEngine.swift:655-664`, `RenderGraph.swift:1152-1162`); Midpoint/Feather fixed at the documented 50/50 defaults giving inner = 0.375, constants shared across paths (`DetailEngine.swift:625-634`, `RenderGraph.swift:1163-1164`). Highlight protection: burn fades by up to 50% above half the default scene white (2.88 linear), in both paths (`DetailEngine.swift:647-671`, `Kernels.swift:686-698` — the kernel previously lacked it entirely, history at `Kernels.swift:675-685`). GPU centers the ellipse on the *crop* rectangle, fixing the sensor-centred bug; straighten is still unaccounted, documented (`RenderGraph.swift:1123-1150,1133-1137`).

**Verdict: DIVERGES from the LR contract by reduction, defensibly.** One slider (EV amount) with four disclosures frozen at defaults — a documented product decision, and the EV denomination + scene-linear placement is a *stronger* invariant than LR's (same burn at every exposure; the "Highlight Priority emerges from pipeline position" claim is real in kind — a scene-linear multiply upstream of the shoulder preserves specular punch-through — but the explicit protection term is doing named work too, so the claim is half-structural, half-tuned; inferred). Falloff is not cos⁴ and does not claim to be. Residual defect: rotated crops get a slightly mis-placed ellipse (self-documented). Both paths pinned to the same constants — cross-path CORRECT.

## Gap List — ranked by daily-edit impact

1. **Sharpen Radius is denominated in render-target pixels** (`RenderGraph.swift:951,982`; `DetailEngine.swift:564,571`). Every sharpened photo is judged on a fit preview whose radius means a different frame-relative width than the export renders. Fix is a `longEdge` scale factor like Masking already has (`RenderGraph.swift:1111-1113`). Highest leverage: silently affects every edit with Amount > 0.
2. **Texture's shipping one-band spectrum vs the reference band-pass — now MEASURED** (`TextureSpectrumProbeTests`, TEXSPEC lines on every pipeline-touching CI run; first data gpu-parity #20). Sinusoids at 2–32 px through both presence stages at Texture +100, RMS-contrast gain per period: reference 2.11/2.09/1.70/1.68/1.51/1.29/1.18/1.08/1.05 at 2/3/4/6/8/12/16/24/32 px; gpu 1.79/1.55/1.62/1.27/1.41/1.21/1.14/1.07/1.05; gpu/ref 0.85/**0.74**/0.95/**0.76**/0.93/0.94/0.97/0.99/1.00. Two findings: the feared finest-scale over-boost is REFUTED — the GPU under-delivers at 2 px (0.85), it does not over-boost noise; and the single band's real divergence is a rippled 24–26% under-delivery notched at 3 px and 6 px (the guided band's response vs the raised-cosine stack's), converging to parity by 16 px. Whether ~three-quarters strength in the 3–6 px octave warrants the M3 band-stack port is now a product-taste call with numbers, not an unknown.
3. **haloSuppression damps the mid-edge slope and misses the rim it exists for — measured** (`DetailEngine.swift:588-592` and `Kernels.swift:192-193`, the same gate in both paths; pinned in `testHaloSuppressionCurrentlyMissesTheRimItExistsToDamp`). The damp is gated on the LOCAL usm magnitude (smoothstep 0.15→0.60 EV), but on a σ1.5-blurred 5.1 EV edge the visible rim sits 2–3 px onto the bright plateau where usm has decayed to ~0.148 — below the floor — while the damp lands mid-edge (usm 0.25–0.33), dulling the slope docs/06 §11.3 says the asymmetry exists to protect. Full deflection delivers ZERO rim reduction. Fix is a designed cross-path change: damp against the local plateau (local-range clamp), constants re-measured, then flip the pinned test to the contract claim.
4. **Capture sharpening: dead Radius control, uncalled and untested RL** (`DetailPanel.swift:60-77,128-134`; `AppleRawSource.swift:165-183`). The docs/06 trust story (measured PSF per frame) does not exist in shipping builds; what ships is an opaque vendor sharpener scaled by one fraction. Either wire `DetailEngine.captureSharpen` into S4 with tests, or remove Radius from the panel. The wiring argument now has a measured row (docs/26 §6): against a known σ1.5 PSF, `captureSharpen` recovers ×1.53 of edge rise at 9.5% overshoot — more recovery than the manual stage at the same dial — and nothing ships it.
5. **Denoise luminance ISO defaults carry the suspected σ double-count that was measured and fixed for chroma** (`DenoiseEngine.swift:1644-1648` vs `1656-1683`; the incriminating luma RMS table at `:574-595`). Every high-ISO import defaults into it. One measurement (the existing `noisyISO6400`-style harness at ISO 25600) either flattens the anchors or acquits them.
6. **Dehaze GPU is missing the sky guard on the positive branch and the skyness term on the negative branch** (`Kernels.swift:135-137,157-161` vs `DetailEngine.swift:417-421,470-471`). Skies — the pixels Dehaze is dragged for — take more correction in every preview/export than the reference defines. Requires handing the kernel the gradient and log-luminance planes the graph already computes.
7. **Clarity's residual one-band rim (0.108/0.092 EV vs the local Laplacian's 0.066/0.050)** and the missing Punch mode (`RenderGraph.swift:565-579`; `DetailEngine.swift:337-340`). Visible only at high Clarity on hard edges; the GPU local Laplacian is the known closure.
8. **Vignette's frozen Midpoint/Feather/Roundness/Highlights and the straighten offset** (`DetailEngine.swift:625-634`; `RenderGraph.swift:1133-1137`). A deliberate reduction, but the first thing an LR refugee will reach for and not find.
9. **Halation is Amount-only** (Size and Redness pinned at `FilmLab.swift:1127-1134`) and the GPU highlight gate is a pedestal stand-in (`FilmLab.swift:560-576`).
10. **Grain has no Roughness axis and a Gaussian-ish plate** where the literature's Boolean model gives crystal-like texture ([Newson et al.](https://www.ipol.im/pub/art/2017/192/)); character vs real scans unmeasured. Lowest urgency: the density-domain envelope, decorrelation, and print anchoring — the parts that read as "film" at normal viewing — are all in place and cross-path pinned.

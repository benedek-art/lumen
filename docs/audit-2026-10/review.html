Fixed

What was broken and is not any more

YOUR WORK · SAFETY

Nothing quietly lost

  • Ingest said "every copy verified" when two identical frames became one file, or when the backup folder was the primary under another name.
  • A darktable sidecar beside a RAW could replace Lumen's own edits.
  • Remounting a drive wiped every photo's signature, EXIF and previews.
  • Saving could downgrade a recipe with heal spots, so an older build would overwrite the healing.
  • A sidecar that failed to save at quit was dropped silently. It is now reported on next launch.
  • The same file opened two ways got two catalog rows, splitting its edits.
THE PICTURE

Pixels that were wrong

  • Colour edits drifted up to 40 codes through an 8-bit colour table. The stage is exact now.
  • Lifting black on a curve turned deep blacks purple on the GPU.
  • GPU halation under-glowed against the reference.
  • Density turned the hue of dark colours; Saturation left a grey drift.
  • Thin brush strokes changed with resolution.
  • Cropped portraits used the landscape frame in the crop and mask tools.
  • A heal spot altered the whole frame on the GPU, and painted heal strokes had the same bug.
FILES · EXPORT · RAW

Getting files in and out

  • Exports to exFAT, FAT and SMB drives failed. They fall back safely now and say why.
  • JPEGs were written at 72 PPI instead of the requested 240, and contact metadata was missing.
  • A truncated RAW or an unsupported Sigma X3F reached the renderer as an infinite image.
  • Files macOS can only open as a thumbnail are refused instead of edited at 296 px.
  • The RAW test corpus now runs on every relevant change; before, it never ran.
USING IT

Interaction and polish

  • Undo during a slider drag saved the undone value.
  • Panel resizing jittered by 10 pt while dragging.
  • Double-click zoomed under the crop tool; the search box queried on every keystroke.
  • Multi-photo crop and straighten used the first photo's frame for all of them.
  • Sliders and the curve editor now work with VoiceOver.
  • Panels now say when the engine is limiting a slider (Zones flattening, eased Highlights).
  • About 34 September audit items closed in two sweeps.
Built

New in the app

macOS-tested

Heal and Clone

Circular spots on Q, source picked automatically, / cycles to the next best source. Painted heal strokes. A dust-finding view. Paste Settings leaves spots behind unless you opt in.

macOS-tested

Creative LUTs

Load a .cube file in the Looks section, at a display or log tap, with an Amount slider. Backed up with the catalog.

needs an HDR screen

HDR preview and export

View ▸ HDR Preview shows the HDR picture on an EDR display. HEIC and JPEG exports can now carry an ISO 21496-1 gain map.

macOS-tested

Culling assists

A sharpness score, burst grouping and closed-eye detection, computed in the background. Sort by sharpness; filter soft, closed-eyes or in-burst frames.

compiled on macOS

Library

Rename and delete albums. Saved smart albums. Keyword hierarchy (Iceland › Reykjavik) and synonyms. Keywords sync both ways with the XMP sidecar.

compiled on macOS

Export naming

{date} is now the capture date, plus {seq}, {camera}, {lens}, {iso} and more. Rename, overwrite or skip on a name clash, with a warning before names collide.

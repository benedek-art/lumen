## 0. Measurement method, stated so it can be checked

**Type advance.** No CoreText on this machine, so widths are estimated two ways and
both are printed wherever a row is near a boundary:

- **flat**: the brief's estimate, **6.2 pt per character at 11 pt** SF Pro (scaled
  linearly for other sizes: 6.77 pt/char at 12 pt).
- **metric**: per-character advances from the Helvetica table × 1.06 (SF Pro Text runs
  ~6 % wider than Helvetica at UI sizes), × 1.06 again for semibold. Over the label
  corpus this averages 6.2 pt/char at 11 pt, i.e. it reproduces the brief's estimate
  while resolving `i`/`l`/`m`/`W` correctly.

Where the two disagree about whether a row fits, the row is marked **borderline** and
both numbers are given. Every number below is in points.

**Type sizes in the code today.** `LumenType.swift:42` — `lumenBody = 12 pt`. Labels
are **12 pt today**, not 11. The approved direction (`PLAN.md` §UI direction item 3)
is **11 pt labels**. So every fit table below is given **at 12 pt (today) and at 11 pt
(after U1)**. Nothing here assumes U1 has landed.

**The horizontal chain** (measured, `file:line`):

| step | value | source |
|---|---|---|
| develop column width `W` | 320 min / 380 default / 520 max | `LumenControls.swift:234-236` |
| scroll inset | −4 each side | `DevelopPanel.swift:510` |
| section card inset | −10 each side | `DevelopColumn.swift:540` |
| **row content width** | **`W − 28`** | = 292 / 352 / 492 |
| extra inset inside a `DevelopDisclosure` | −8 **leading only** | `DevelopPanel.swift:245` |

| **slider track** | **`W − 170`** (top level) / **`W − 178`** (in a disclosure) | derived |

**Scrollbar**: `.scrollIndicators(.never)` (`DevelopPanel.swift:513`), so no legacy
15 pt scroller inset. Confirmed; the chain above is the whole of it.

## 1. THE LABEL COLUMN — every row, measured

`Lumen.labelWidth = 86` is a **fixed frame** (`LumenControls.swift:474-477`,
`LumenMenu.swift:381`, `CropPanel.swift:410/504/536/554/574`, `EffectsPanel.swift:511`,
`LookPanel.swift:1336`). **It does not vary with column width.** Therefore *label
truncation is identical at 320, 380 and 520* — the column width buys track, never
label. This is the single most useful fact for U2 and it is worth stating in the
landing: widening the column does not fix a truncated label; only `labelWidth` or the
label text does.

`LumenSlider` applies `.lineLimit(1)` + `.minimumScaleFactor(0.86)`
(`LumenControls.swift:478`, `:488`), so:

- **shrinks** when nominal width > 86 (renders below 12 pt / 11 pt),
- **truncates** when nominal width × 0.86 > 86, i.e. nominal > **100.0**.

`LumenMenuPicker` (`LumenMenu.swift:384-385`) has the same pair.
`PrinterLightRow` (`LookPanel.swift:1336-1337`) has `lineLimit(1)` and **no**
`minimumScaleFactor` → truncates the moment nominal > 86.
`CropPanel`'s five label `Text`s (`:408-410, :502-504, :534-536, :552-554, :572-574`)
have **neither** → they push the row or wrap.

## 3. THE TRACK — what each column width actually buys

`LumenControls.swift:221-228` states the design threshold — "under the ~1.0 at which a

records as the state in which "58 of the 201 integer values could not be landed on by
dragging at all". See finding G1-02.

## 4. ROW PITCH — three pitches, none of them 24

Owner's decision: **24 pt, one pitch everywhere** (`PLAN.md`, Decisions table).
Measured today, all panels stack rows at `VStack(spacing: 2)` (31 of 33 stacks in the
six panel files; the other two are `spacing: 4`):

|---|---|---|---|---|

`BasicPanel.swift:373` (WB preset row), `DetailPanel.swift:137` (capture badge),
`DetailPanel.swift:298` (Lightroom-defaults hint), `DetailPanel.swift:361` (ISO badge),

The accordion draws a `LumenSectionHeader` per section in `WorkspaceSectionView`
(`DevelopColumn.swift:510-517`). A panel that then draws its own `DevelopSection` for
the *same* name prints the heading twice. `BasicPanel.swift:118-126` and
`LookPanel.swift:289-296` both record this as a defect that was fixed by dropping the

| section | card header (`Workspace.swift:206-222`) | inner header | file:line |
|---|---|---|---|
| `.frame` | **"CROP"** | `DevelopSection("Crop", …)` — **"CROP"** | `EffectsPanel.swift:92` → `CropPanel.swift:312` |
| `.optics` | "LENS" | `DevelopSection("Lens Corrections", …)` | `EffectsPanel.swift:93` → `EffectsPanel.swift:405` |
| `.softProof` | "SOFT PROOF" | *correctly guarded* — `if only == nil { proofSection } else { proofRows }` | `EffectsPanel.swift:95-97` |

`.frame` is the S2: two identical headings one row apart, **each with its own chevron,
its own modified dot and its own Reset** — and the two Resets have different scopes
(`WorkspaceSection.frame.reset` vs `CropPanel.resetGeometry()`,
`CropPanel.swift:313-316`). See finding G1-03.

**Heading orphaned from its content — `EXPORT RECIPES`.** `ExportRecipesSection`

sheet.")` + a text button. `DevelopNote.body` is `if prominent { … }`

(`DevelopPanel.swift:279`), so **the sentence is never drawn**. The section renders as a
heading over a bare accent-coloured "Open Export…" link. See finding G1-04.

| `Display Transform` | 155.7 | — | fits | fits | fits |

## 8. FINDINGS

### G1-01 — Two Detail labels truncate inside the fixed 86 pt label column, at every column width
severity: S2
confidence: measured
class: defect
size: S
evidence: `LumenControls.swift:265` `static let labelWidth: CGFloat = 86`, applied at
`:474-477` as a fixed `.frame(width:)` with `.lineLimit(1)` (`:478`) and
`.minimumScaleFactor(0.86)` (`:488`). Truncation threshold = 86 / 0.86 = **100.0 pt**.
Measured at 12 pt: `Luminance Contrast` (`DetailPanel.swift:452`) = **113.8** metric /
121.9 flat; `Colour Smoothness` (`DetailPanel.swift:490`) = **111.7** / 115.1;
`Halo Suppression` (`DetailPanel.swift:266`) = **100.4** / 108.3 (borderline). At the
approved 11 pt the first two are still 104.3 and 102.4 → **89.7 and 88.1 after shrink,
still over 86**. The label frame does not scale with the column, so 320, 380 and 520 are
identical. `LumenControls.swift:479-487` asserts the opposite: "a name that renders 8 %
smaller on four rows out of ninety-two".
how the owner sees it: in Detail → Noise Reduction, two rows read `Luminance Contra…`
and `Colour Smoothn…` at a visibly smaller size than the rows above them, at every
window size, and widening the panel does not help.
fix: `Sources/LumenApp/DetailPanel.swift` — rename under the masters they already sit
below (`Luminance Contrast` → `Contrast`, `Colour Smoothness` → `Smoothness`), **or**
`LumenControls.swift:265` → `labelWidth = 104` at 11 pt and accept 18 pt off every
track. THE TEST: a `LumenAppTests` case that asserts, for a table of every slider title
shipped in the panel files, `NSAttributedString(string: title, attributes: [.font:
NSFont.systemFont(ofSize: 11)]).size().width * 0.86 <= Lumen.labelWidth`. Substitute
either label back and it goes red.

What you should see now

Things that should feel different in this build. If one doesn't, that's a finding worth telling me.

Viewing and editing

  • Pinch from fit: the photo grows smoothly from under your fingers, with no shrink-then-jump.
  • Lift your fingers: nothing moves, even when the sharp version arrives.
  • Double-click or Z to 1:1: one image pixel per screen pixel, sharp.
  • Drag Exposure, Saturation, Texture, Temp or a mixer slider: the photo follows your hand. Panels outside the dragged slider catch up just after you let go.
  • Film grain at any amount: no stripes, no repeating tile, and 100% matches the export.

Masks, files and cards

  • Create a mask, then move one of its sliders: the red disappears and stays gone until you hover the row.
  • Mask edits with several photos selected only change the photo you're on.
  • Duplicate a mask as its inverse: no halo along the edge.
  • Rename a shoot folder in Finder: its edits, snapshots and albums come back with it.
  • Ingest a card with video on it: the sheet names what it won't copy and keeps Eject locked. The card itself can't be picked as the destination.
  • A Leica Monochrom or Fujifilm X-H2 RAW tells you why it won't open, instead of showing black or nothing.

Avoid these until the next run fixes them

  • Culling in the grid right after opening a folder. Keys can go into the "New album" field (one test created an album called "px3"). Cull from the loupe.
  • Culling with a filter on. A hidden photo can still take the keys; a pick was lost this way.
  • Pressing ' outside masking. It inverts the selected photo's mask and changes its pixels.
  • The ⇧H raw-truth panel. It overstates clipping 6 to 10× and called a recoverable Panasonic shot "not recoverable".
  • Colour film stocks on saturated subjects or darker skin. They clip colours to a zero channel.
  • Compare's "1:1". It isn't 1:1, and the two panes sit at different magnifications.
  • Formatting a card until you've seen the new ingest sheet name every file it left behind.

Open findings, ranked by harm

The top 15 of about 110. Each has a repro file and a run ID in docs/agent-runs/findings/. The next run starts at number 1.

Culling keys go to the wrong place Blocker

Keys type into "New album" at launch; a filter leaves a hidden photo as the target (a pick was lost); keys act with the filter popover open; ⌘K once missed focus and culled three photos.

' outside masking inverts a mask Blocker

Proven in the app: the picture changed visibly. Fix written on the keymap branch, waiting on its re-run.

Exposure instruments mislead Major

⇧H treats 1.0 as sensor white and overstates clipping 6–10×; histogram and J count about half the blown area; ] can't show highlight detail; J shows only all-channel clipping.

Colour film stocks clip and treat skin unevenly Major

15–26% of a colour chart clips to a zero channel; Portra pushes darker skin orange and lighter skin pale; Tri-X can't be toned.

Grading and colour controls misbehave Major

Shadows wheel only reaches near-black; Point Colour hue shifts spill onto other colours; B&W bands at −100 go to black; wheels change brightness.

Tone controls Major

Zone sliders crush 1–3 stops into one grey; Tint goes blue/olive instead of magenta/green; Protect Skin misses golden and olive skin; Auto Tone wrecks dark photos. Fixes built, 10 tests still failing.

Heal can invent colours Major

A spot on a black card came out navy; edge spots tint; strokes at the edge bring scratches back. Clone is fine.

Ingest edge cases Major

Re-ingest after an in-camera delete copies frames again under new names; with renaming off, main copy and backup can hold different photos under one name.

Compare and before/after views Major

Compare's 1:1 is wrong and uneven; ⇧Y split at 1:1 is blank; side-by-side panes are off-centre.

Masking leftovers Major

O never returns to Auto; People mask selected 44% of a photo with no person; a drag 33 pt from a gradient replaces it.

Denoise at fit and on Nikons Major

The fit view never runs Classic NR; Nikon noise is 1.6–2.1× what the model assumes; a D5600 night frame's shadows go magenta.

Viewer doesn't match export on some files Major

The A7 IV viewer is softer and hazier. Fix built (4.2 → 1.5 levels) but it slows first view per photo. Your call.

Export details Medium

8-bit Adobe RGB/ProPhoto band in shadows; lens serial leaks with serial off; TIFF/PNG may carry an alpha channel; one export toggle does nothing.

Lag when changing photos in Develop Medium

3 freezes of 95–360 ms per photo change; first drag of each slider compiles a GPU program.

Engine edge cases Medium

Out-of-gamut blues shift toward violet; grade table bands at narrow pivots; local curve above Amount 100 inverts; crops export 1 px too big.

Decisions waiting on you

Taste calls the agents were told not to make. My recommendation is in brackets where there is one.

How photos should look

  • Saturation +100 darkens strong colours 15% on average, up to 40% for blues (bring it down to Vibrance's level).
  • Sharpening: crisp but 1.5–5% brighter on dark foliage, or exact but softer (crisp, re-measured).
  • Zone fix: pushing one zone hard moves its neighbours, or the slider stops working past about +0.9 EV (keep the spill).
  • Raised Black target: saturated colours fade to the same grey floor as neutrals (keep).
  • Grain amplitude ended 2–8% lower after the seam fix. Does it still feel right?
  • Clarity −100 is barely visible; Whites −100 and Blacks +100 are weak.
  • Matte print sharpening may be twice what's usual (check a real print first).

How the app should behave

  • Viewer accuracy vs about 2 s slower first view per photo on the test Mac.
  • Export Overwrite: files exported before the fix would get renamed past instead of replaced.
  • A deleted folder re-imported with identical files gets its old edits back (keep).
  • Soft preview while dragging capture sharpening or AI denoise, vs sharp but steppy (soft).
  • Sort ties: newest file first, or capture time (capture time).
  • Rendering downsized exports at delivery size, which would also stop the GPU memory failures.
